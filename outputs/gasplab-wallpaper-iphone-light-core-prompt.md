# GaspLab — Light Core

Wallpaper vertical criado com a ferramenta integrada image_gen, a partir do símbolo oficial fornecido. Composição com espaço escuro no topo para o relógio. Ajustar o enquadramento ao aplicar, conforme o modelo do iPhone.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE extraordinary premium iPhone wallpaper, ultra-tall portrait aspect ratio 9:19.5, ideally 1296x2808. Full bleed finished wallpaper, absolutely no phone mockup, no interface, no clock, no captions, no wordmark. Input image is the official GaspLab symbol, the geometry and brand identity reference. Creative concept: 'BLUEPRINT / LIGHT CORE' — the angular GaspLab emblem becomes a monumental precision-cut translucent cobalt optical-glass sculpture suspended in an infinite almost-black navy void. Make this a sophisticated collectible digital artwork, visually arresting, tactile, elegant, and highly art-directed. The recognizable front silhouette must faithfully follow the supplied blocky open G and its separate cyan square at the upper right; do not invent another logo. Mostly frontal with only a subtle three-quarter tilt so the G remains instantly recognizable, modest deep extrusion, surgical crisp chamfered edges, layers of clear cobalt glass with a brushed dark titanium inner spine, believable optical refraction and brilliant narrow azure caustics. The separate cyan square floats a tiny distance away at exactly the source logo's upper-right position like the intelligence core; it emits controlled pale-blue light into the glass. Main sculpture centered horizontally at x=50%, centered vertically at y=59%, overall width about 66% of canvas and height about 31% of canvas, maintaining its roughly square silhouette. Keep the whole upper 30% quiet near-black navy with just the faintest atmosphere to leave ample legible space for the iPhone lock screen clock. Bottom 14% also very quiet for controls. One extremely delicate elliptical blue-white filament travels diagonally behind the sculpture and reappears in front at the lower edge, with physical occlusion and subtle depth of field, suggesting engineered flow rather than a sci-fi portal. Beneath the emblem, restrained light caustics dissolve into velvet-black darkness; tiny architectural construction marks may appear only close to the object, barely visible. No busy grid. Palette strictly GaspLab: midnight #070A12, deep #0B1530, cobalt #4D7CFF, signal azure #6CC0FF, pinprick ice highlights #ECF2FF. Deep OLED-style blacks, dramatic controlled studio lighting, high material realism, rich optical depth, polished modern industrial design meets museum light installation. High contrast localized to the sculpture, generous negative space, meticulous finish. Avoid generic cyberpunk, neon overload, purple, green, rainbow colors, stars, planets, smoke clouds, gaming aesthetic, text, people, extra logos, tiny fake technical labels, watermarks, borders. Render large and exceptionally sharp.
